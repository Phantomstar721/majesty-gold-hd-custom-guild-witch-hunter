# Custom Guild: Witch Hunters

A new hero guild for **Majesty Gold HD**. Recruit Witch Hunters who fight with hand crossbows, hatchets and silver. Unlock new equipment at the Argent Crucible, rally heroes against lairs, and send Solarii into battle on a golden comet.

## Chapterhouse

Build the Chapterhouse at Castle level 3 to recruit Witch Hunters. They mark their quarry, change weapons when enemies close in and learn new abilities as they level up.

| Level | Ability |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Hunter’s Mark** lowers the target’s Dodge and Parry. |
| 3 | **Silver Bolt** burns and dispels eligible foes. |
| 5 | **Sundering Shot** increases damage the target takes. **Weakening Strike** reduces damage the target deals. Only one strike effect applies at a time. |
| 8 | **Witch Ward** protects the Hunter against her marked quarry. |

**Breach the Den** costs 500 gold and rallies heroes against a monster lair for three minutes. Cast it from the Chapterhouse at level 2 or from the sovereign spell bar with a completed level 3 Chapterhouse. Level 6 Solarii can learn **Dawnfall** while a completed level 3 Chapterhouse stands. With it, they can leap to a breached lair or an enemy marked by a Witch Hunter.

## Argent Crucible

Build one Crucible per kingdom. Employ a living level 8 Witch Hunter to unlock its services. The hired Hunter leaves adventuring permanently. You can upgrade the building before hiring.

- **Nightglass Lenses** improve sight, **Windlass Assembly** extends crossbow range, and **Gravebind Silver** adds a slow to Hunter’s Mark.
- **Silver Salts** cost 60 gold. A hero can carry a dose, use it to cure Ratman Plague and gain 60 seconds of immunity.
- At level 3, **Purge** kills infected kingdom units and **Cleanse** cures them. Either action takes 30 simulation seconds and starts a shared five-day recharge. Cleanse requires compatible Alchemist Lab support, a completed level 3 Laboratory with Arcane Infusion, and an upfront payment of 5,000 gold and five reagents. Alchemist Lab is optional for the rest of the mod.

## Installation

1. Install the playable Witch Hunters package through Steam Workshop.
2. Open [Majesty Mod Manager from Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793024054) and install the required helpers if prompted.
3. Select Witch Hunters, choose **Prepare Selected Mods**, and launch Majesty through the Manager.

Use Mod Manager 0.3.15 beta or a compatible build. Start a new game when adding the guild to your mod list.

This repository contains the guild’s gameplay source and configuration. The playable package includes the compiled resources and is distributed through Steam Workshop.
